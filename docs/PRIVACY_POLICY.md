# Micro-SaaS Scout Privacy Policy Template

Last updated: [DATE]

Micro-SaaS Scout helps users analyze websites and discover Micro-SaaS opportunities using AI.

## Information we collect

When you click **Analyze current website**, the extension may collect:

- Current page URL
- Page title
- Meta tags
- Headings
- Visible page text
- Calls to action
- Pricing signals
- Form labels
- Review/testimonial text
- Links
- Optional page screenshot for UI/UX analysis

For trial and license management, the extension may also store or process:

- Local device ID generated by the extension
- Trial start date and trial end date
- License/unlock status
- Email address if you enter it for payment receipt or unlock support
- Payment reference ID when provided by Razorpay or another payment provider

We do not collect this information until you intentionally run an analysis.

## How we use information

We use collected website information to:

- Generate AI website analysis reports
- Identify missing features and business opportunities
- Estimate revenue potential
- Suggest MVP roadmaps and tech stacks
- Power the AI chat assistant
- Save reports you choose to save

## AI providers

Website analysis may be processed by configured AI providers such as OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic Claude through our secure backend.

API keys are stored only on the backend and are never included in the Chrome extension.

## Data storage

Reports may be stored:

- Locally in Chrome extension storage
- In our Supabase database if you save a report while signed in

Trial and license data may be stored:

- Locally in Chrome extension storage
- In our backend database to verify lifetime unlock access

## Payments

Payments may be processed by Razorpay, Stripe, or another configured payment provider. We do not store full credit card numbers. Payment processors may collect payment details according to their own privacy policies.

## Data sharing

We do not sell user data.

We share data only with service providers needed to operate the app:

- AI providers for report generation
- Supabase for database storage
- Clerk for authentication
- Razorpay and/or Stripe for payments
- Hosting providers such as Vercel and Render
